The first weeks

Trackers built against the market.

Feed-tracking apps sell targets, streaks and red numbers to people who have not slept in nine days. There is published evidence that intensive tracking goes hand in hand with worse postnatal anxiety. So Beavo tracks sleep, feeds and nappies with nothing that can be failed.

Sleep · pausable, day and night split

Feeds and nappies · no schedule

  • Nodaily target, because the NHS asks you to feed when the baby asks, not when an app does.
  • Noaverage that counts today's half-finished day against you. Only a completed day is measured.
  • Yesnappy counts checked against NHS guidance for your baby's age — the one number that genuinely matters in week one.
  • Yesa pause button, because a session that gets interrupted is not a session you failed.

And if it ends.

A pregnancy app that keeps counting is unbearable, and deleting it deletes him from a thing that happened to him too. So Beavo has a switch. One tap takes the whole app quiet — the week counter, the notifications, right down to the icon on the home screen. Nothing you saved is deleted. You can turn it back on.

Important safety

Beavo provides general education and planning support. It does not diagnose conditions and does not replace advice from a midwife, maternity unit, GP or emergency service. For reduced or changed baby movements, bleeding, severe pain, waters breaking with concerning symptoms or any urgent concern, contact the maternity unit or NHS 111. Call 999 in an emergency.
